The Bøgh Line

Eight generations from Søren Jensen of Fredenborg, documented in the Jerslev register in 1698 and 1702, to Jens Kristian Marinus Thomsen Bøgh, born at Klæstrup in 1903. The whole line lived within a 25 km stretch of central Vendsyssel, and after two centuries it came back to the parish it started in. This page companions Finding Fredenborg, which locates the house where it begins.

The pattern of movement

Five moves in eight generations, all short, all within the same group of neighbouring parishes. Total distance about 56 km over two hundred years.

  • Fredenborg, Svennum → Vester Brønderslev, c. 1700–1724 (reported): 10 km.
  • Vester Brønderslev → Hellevad, 1724–1774 (reported): 13 km.
  • Hellevad → Lille Nejsum, by 1810: 13 km.
  • Lille Nejsum → Skæve, 1814–1853: 4 km.
  • Skæve → Klæstrup, 1850–1876: 16 km.

Each step is to an adjoining parish, which is what a family of cottagers and smallholders in this period typically did: marriages, leases and farm service were found within walking distance, and a move of one parish was a big one. The return to Klæstrup in 1876 closes a loop. Klæstrup lies 4 km west of Fredenborg, in the same village fields that Anders of Fredenborg was harvesting in 1676.

What would strengthen the line

  1. Christen Sørensen’s burial, Jerslev, February 1746. Would confirm the reported death date, give his age, and show whether the scribe still wrote “Fredenborg”.
  2. The 1724 baptism of Peder. Vester Brønderslev was in the Jerslev pastorate, so the entry may be in either church’s book. The father’s name as written decides whether Peder is Christen’s son (Peder Christensen) or something else.
  3. Søren Jensen’s burial. Age at death gives his birth year and settles whether the Peder Sørensen buried in 1720, aged 49, could be his son.
  4. Christen Sørensen’s marriage, Jerslev or Vester Brønderslev, c. 1720–1724. Names his wife and her home, and may explain the move toward Vester Brønderslev.
  5. Simon Pedersen’s baptism, Hellevad c. 1774. His three marriages (1810, 1812, 1813) are in the Skæve register; the baptism would name his parents and close the gap to Peder.
  6. The 1787, 1801 and 1834 censuses for Hellevad and Skæve, which list households by place and would fix where Simon and Thomas lived between baptisms.
