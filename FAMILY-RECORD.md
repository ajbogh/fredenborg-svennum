# Family record: working notes

family history page can be written from sources rather than memory. Items marked **[verified]**
have been read from the original image. Items marked **[reported]** come from the family record on
Ancestry and have not yet been checked. Items marked **[hypothesis]** are inferences.

### Generation 3: Peder Jørgensen **[verified]** — formerly recorded as "Peder Sørensen"
- The 1787 census (Raunsholt, Skæve) wrote him "Peder Sørensen, 63". This was an enumerator's error. The Hellevad register and the 1801 census both give **Peder Jørgensen**.
- Baptised Jerslev 6 Nov 1729: "Peder, Jørgen Pedersens og Mette Pedersdatters i Villestrup". The scribe first wrote "Christen Jensens", struck it, and wrote "Jørgen Pedersen" above. Godparents: Christen i Sigaard, Jens i Høholt, Peder i Falden, Christens hustru i Krattet, Maren Jensdatter paa Veiene. (DFS kbid821338; image 14415867.) Identification with the Hellevad groom rests on the name, the 1801 age (71 → b. 1730) and the absence of any other Peder Jørgensen born 1720–1740 in indexed Hjørring amt registers. **[strong, one step short of direct proof]**
- Betrothed 11 Nov 1760, married 15 Dec 1760 at Hellevad to Inger Christensdatter: "ung Karl Peder Jørgensen af Nørkier[?] med Pigen Inger Christensdatter ibid."
- Resident at Hørkied (Hørkiær) in Kirkeskov, Hellevad parish, 1761–1763.
- At Raunsholt, Skæve, by 1787 (retired, son Christen holds the farm) and 1801 (husbonde again, aged 71, "bonde og gårdbeboer", one marriage).
- Death not yet found; after 1 Feb 1801, probably Skæve.
- Children: Jørgen (bapt. 8 Mar 1761, Hellevad; godparents Jens Andersen af Fodtved, Christen Christensen af Kirkeskov, Simon Christensen af Allerop; absent 1787/1801); Christen (bapt. 20 Nov 1763, Hellevad; carried by Maren Hansdatter fra Brønhavnen[?] i Voer Sogn; same three godfathers; farmer at Raunsholt 1787, single at home 1801); Mads (c. 1767); Anne Kristine (c. 1771); Mette Marie (c. 1774–76; at home 1801 as Mette, 27); Simon (c. 1777–78).
- 1801 household also: Christen Laursen, 32, married farmhand (possible son-in-law); Christen Christensen, 20; Jens Jensen, 12; Ane Jensdatter, 20, servants.

### Generation 2: Jørgen Pedersen and Mette Pedersdatter of Villestrup **[verified; patronymic 2:1]**
- **12 May 1726, Jerslev burials: "Jørgens Pedersens hustru i Villestrup", aged 70** (image 14415960_R). A Jørgen Pedersen of Villestrup was widowed 17 months before the 1727 marriage. Either our Jørgen was an old widower (b. c. 1650s, before the register; would explain the missing baptism), or Store and Lille Villestrup held two Jørgen Pedersens. The 1727 marriage entry does not say enkemand. Decide by his burial with age (not in Jerslev to 1743) or Villestrup baptisms 1684–1700 to a Jørgen Pedersen. **[open]**
- Betrothed 13 Apr 1727, married 26 Oct 1727, Jerslev. The marriage entry writes the groom **"Jørgen Jensen"**; both baptisms (1728, 1729) write **"Jørgen Pedersen"**. Taken as Pedersen on the two baptisms, with the 1727 form recorded as a scribal variant until a fourth record settles it. No home farm given for either party. (Image 14415903.)
- Children:
  - **Kirsten**, bapt. 11 Feb 1728, Villestrup. Godparents: Jens i Kiøbakken, Christen Christensen i Jerslev, Peder Mylund i Jerslev, Christen Pedersens hustru i Krattet, Maren Pedersdatter i Smikken. (Image 14415865.) **Buried 15 Jun 1738**, "Jørgen Pedersens datter Kirsten i Villestrup", aged about 11. (Image 14415964.)
  - **Peder**, bapt. 6 Nov 1729, Villestrup (scribe first wrote "Christen Jensens", struck, corrected to Jørgen Pedersen). Godparents: Christen i Sigaard, Jens i Høholt, Peder i Falden, Christens hustru i Krattet, Maren Jensdatter paa Veiene. (Image 14415867.)
  - No further children in Jerslev baptisms Jan 1727 – Feb 1733 (read in full).
- Resident at Villestrup 1728 to at least June 1738.
- Not buried in Jerslev 1725–1744 (read in full); deaths later.
- **Jørgen Pedersen's origin**: NOT baptised in Jerslev. Every Jerslev baptism Jan 1690 – Dec 1707 has been read; the only Jørgens are Morten Jensen's paa Vejen (24 Aug 1690) and Søren Jørgensen's (1694). The book is the combined Jerslev–Vester Brønderslev register (Hjulskov farms of Vester Brønderslev parish appear throughout), so Vester Brønderslev is covered too. Born c. 1695–1705 outside the parish, or in the lost Hellevad gap (1673–1732). Father a Peder by patronymic; nothing more.
- **Mette Pedersdatter's origin**: **baptised 24 May 1705, Jerslev, "Mette, Peder Pedersens i Sterup"**. Godparents: Jens Pedersen i Vester Mølle, Peder Jensen Møller, Peder Jensen i Jerslef, Anne Sørensdatter i Kraggaarden, Maren Tomsdatter ibid. (Image 14415798_L.) An **elder sister, also Mette**, was baptised 21 May 1702, "Mette, Peder Pedersens i Sterup"; godparents Jens Pedersen i Vester Mølle, Ole Tomsen i Sterup, Laurs Pedersen paa Heden, Mikkel Christensens hustru i Vester Mølle, Kirstine Laursdatter i Sterup (image 14415791_R). Reuse of the name in 1705 implies the 1702 child died young, though her burial was not spotted in a skim of 1702–05. Either Mette fits the 1727 bride (aged 25 or 22); both are daughters of Peder Pedersen and Karen Jensdatter. Identity rests on name, parish and age fit; no later record names her father. **[strong]**
- **Krattet, final reading.** The Krattet godmother of 1728–29 (Christen Pedersen's wife) was first read as Jørgen's sister-in-law, then as Mette's. Both readings are withdrawn. Krattet was Peder Sørensen's household (daughter Magdalena bapt. 4 Dec 1701; son Christen bapt. 26 Jul 1722, wife Maren Christensdatter; Christen Pedersen and Jens Pedersen of Krattet, godfathers 1722, fit as his grown sons). Jørgen is absent from the Jerslev baptisms in Peder Sørensen's child-bearing years, and Mette was baptised from Sterup to a Peder Pedersen, not from Krattet to Peder Sørensen. Krattet and Sterup are adjacent; the godmother is a neighbour. The one loose end: **Peder Pedersen "af Krattet", buried 21 Jun 1739, no age** (image 14415964), could be Mette's father moved from Sterup late in life. Unproven.
- Villestrup: farm in Jerslev parish east of Sterup, 1662 "Willestrup". Neighbouring godparent farms: Sigård, Høholt, Falden, Krattet, Vejen, Smikken, Kiøbakken (Kjøbakken).
- Ancestry hint "Jørgen Pedersen b. 1689 Elsted, Århus, son of Peder Sørensen and Mette Clementsdatter" rejected: 170 km away and a decade too old for a first marriage in 1727.

Christensdatter i Sterup; son Peder bapt. 23 Dec 1703 (godparents Niels Pedersen, Jens Jensen, Peder Pedersen, all of Sterup; Anne Christensdatter); godfather 16 Dec 1703 to Niels Pedersen's son. So "Raunsholt" is a byname for a man who had come from there; he was b. c. 1640s–1650 (wife b. c. 1650), the older generation. Link to the 1787 family unproven; test in Skæve register 1675– for Laurs Pedersen at Raunsholt. NB a second Raunsholt exists in Åsted parish, Horns herred (Ravnsholt farm 57.4321, 10.4071, 1.3 km SSW of Åsted church, 23 km NE of Sterup); 1787 household Jens Olufsen 60 & Helle Thomasdatter 67, unrelated. The 1787 index has only these two Raunsholt households: Skæve (ours) and Åsted. Laurs's byname could come from either.; child of Laurs Pedersen buried 1 Jul 1714 at "Torlak"(?). Jerslev burials 1706–1743 read: no burial for Laurs Pedersen of Sterup. Related: **13 Jan 1732 "Lauritz Pedersen paa Søerheden", 62** (b. c. 1670), probably the "Laurs Pedersen paa Heden" godfather of 1702; same generation as the Sterup brothers, possibly one of them who moved. 17 Mar 1737 Laurs Andersen i Sterup, 62 (not a Pedersen). **12 Feb 1719 "Jens Pedersens hustru i Vester Sterups Mølle", 73** (b. c. 1646): very probably Karen Jensdatter's mother. **22 Mar 1724 "Jens Pedersen i Vester Mølle", 83** (b. c. 1641), image 14415959_R: very probably Karen Jensdatter's father, godfather to both Mettes (1702, 1705). Peder (b. c. 1670–78) and Laurs (married 1702) are the same generation, so they read as brothers, sons of old Peder Pedersen (c. 1625–1705). No record links Laurs to Jørgen Pedersen of Villestrup.

### Generation 8: Jens Kristian Marinus Thomsen Bøgh **[verified: baptism 1903, census 1911]**
- **Baptism**: Jerslev register, born males 1903 no. 8, p. 25 (Ancestry 48491_b411262-0025a). Born 15 Feb 1903, Klæstrup; baptised 9 Apr 1903 in church. "Jens Kristian Marinus Thomsen [struck] Bøgh". Margin: **"Navnet Bøgh i Henhold til Navnebevilling af 26/2 1906."** Surname Bøgh by name licence 26 Feb 1906.
- **1906 census (1 Feb 1906), Skæve parish, Dronninglund herred, schema 23** (Rigsarkivet image 353860): at **Tuen, matr. 29** (the farm beside Søndre Raunsholt), household of gaardejer Niels Peter Martinus Madsen: **"Jens Kristjan Marjus Thomsen", M, b. 7/9 1889 (last digit possibly 7), unmarried, tjenestetyende** (servant), and **"Martin Kristjan Thomsen", M, b. 4/4 1891, unmarried, servant** (probable brother). Still Thomsen, not Bøgh, in 1906.
- CONFLICT: the family record gives Jens Kristian Marinus b. 1903, son of Severin (b. 1876). A man b. 1889 cannot be Severin's son. Either (a) Marius b. 1889 is Christen Thomsen's son and Severin's brother, and the 1903 Marinus is a nephew named for him, or (b) the family record has merged two people. The archive's A3111 creator is "Marius Bøgh", matching the 1889 man's name. RESOLVED (researcher, Oct 2026): the map is credited to "Marinus Bøgh" and Marinus b. 1903 is the only Marinus in the family, so he drew it (archive date 1999 may be the drawing or the registration date). The 1906 Skæve servant Jens Kristian Marius Thomsen b. 1889 is therefore a different man, most likely Christen Thomsen's son and Marinus's uncle, with Martin Kristian b. 1891 another uncle. Marinus was probably named for him. The A3111 donor "Marius Bøgh" could be either man. To confirm: Marius's 1889 baptism (parents) and Severin's 1906 Klæstrup household.
- Drew the map of Klæstrup from memory (record dated 1999). Archive A3111 (archival creator "Marius Bøgh"): Klæstrup School history 1847–1962 and 1960 list of Klæstrup residents; possibly his.
