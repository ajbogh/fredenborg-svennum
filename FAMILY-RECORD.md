# Family record: working notes

Running notes of every person, place and connection established in this research, kept so the
family history page can be written from sources rather than memory. Items marked **[verified]**
have been read from the original image. Items marked **[reported]** come from the family record on
Ancestry and have not yet been checked. Items marked **[hypothesis]** are inferences.

## People, by generation

### Generation 1: Søren Jensen **[verified]**
- Resident at Fredenborg, Svennum, Jerslev parish. "Sørens udj Fredenborg" 13 Feb 1698; "Søren Jensen Fredenborg" 10 Sep 1702.
- Children: Christen (bapt. 13 Feb 1698), Jens (bapt. 10 Sep 1702).
- Possibly the same as "Søren Jensen i Knæppen i Svennum" who baptised a son Jens 25 Nov 1708 (Knæppen a locality in Svennum).
- Not an Andersen brother; probably entered the house by marriage. **[hypothesis]**
- Possible father: Jens Sørensen, co-tenant of Svennum farm no. 6 in 1688, perhaps the Jens Sørensen who was farmhand to Anders and Niels Pedersen of Jerslev in 1653. **[hypothesis]**
- Birth and death not found. Burials to 1725 have no Søren Jensen of Fredenborg.

### The Andersen household at Fredenborg, 1676–1699 **[verified]**
- Anders "i Fredenborg", 1676 tithe case. Probably Anders Pedersen of Jerslev (fl. 1632–1654), son of Peder Pedersen, estate agent for the Kaas family. **[hypothesis]**
- Peder Andersen, 1676 Trøgdrup summons; child Niels buried 15 Jan 1688 "i Fredenborg i Svennum".
- Hans Andersen, son Christen (9) buried 1 Dec 1689 "i Fredenborg".
- Niels Andersen, tenant of Svennum no. 8 (the house) and no. 3 in 1688; byname "Niels Fredenborg" 1692, 1698; wife Maren Nielsdatter (36) buried 26 Sep 1689; children buried 1688, 1692, 1698; he himself probably the Niels Andersen "i Jerslef" buried 25 Dec 1719 aged 82 with wife Helle Jensdatter (55).
- Jens Andersen, tenant of Svennum no. 4 in 1688.
- Niels Tomsen, tenant of Svennum no. 2 in 1688; "Niels Tomsen Fredenborren" buried 7 May 1699 aged 80.
- Palle Nielsen "i Fredenborg", godfather 28 Jun 1704.

### Generation 2: Christen Sørensen **[baptism verified]**
- Baptised Jerslev 13 Feb 1698, son of Søren of Fredenborg. Godparents: Knud Kampmann (of Kølskegård, 1666–1704, son of the priest of Aggersborg), Kield Andersøn (possibly Kjeld Nors, parish clerk), Lars Nielsen (probably tenant of Svennum no. 5), Marie Fischer the priest's wife (1652–1717, wife of Frederik Boysen, priest 1684–1699), Dorthe Jensdatter (of the clerk's house).
- Death 20 Feb 1746 at "Fredensborg" **[reported]**: NOT in the Jerslev burials for 1744–1747. The report must come from another parish's register and another Fredensborg. Unresolved.

### Generation 3: Peder Sørensen **[reported, contradicted]**
- Reported born 1724, Vester Brønderslev, son of Christen Sørensen. Patronymic inconsistent (would be Christensen).
- Jerslev baptisms 1723–1725 have no Peder with father Christen or Søren. The only Peder of 1724 (21 May) is illegitimate, mother Maren Christensdatter of Søheden, father Rasmus Jensen, farmhand at Dal.
- Vester Brønderslev's own baptisms for 1722–1725 (same book, images 14415829–30) have no matching Peder either.
- Peder Sørensen of Svennum, 49, buried 17 Mar 1720 (born c. 1671): possibly Søren Jensen's son. **[hypothesis]**
- Status: this generation is unproven. Simon Pedersen's baptism, once found, will name his father and resolve it.

### Generation 4: Simon Pedersen **[1814, 1834 verified; birth open]**
- Gaardmand (farmer) at Lille Nejsum, Skæve parish.
- Ages: 1834 census 60 (b. 1774); 1840 census 63, 1845 68, 1850 73, burial 1853 76 (all b. 1777). Birth year taken as 1777.
- Birthplace per 1845 and 1850 censuses: Hellevad sogn.
- Baptism NOT found in Hellevad 1776–Sep 1778, nor in Skæve 1776–Oct 1779. Ørum and Hellum pages of the Hellevad book not yet read; years before 1776 not read.
- Candidate: 1787 census, Raunsholt (Ravnsholt), Skæve: Simon Pedersen, 9, "deres børn" (born c. 1778). Parents' names on that page not yet read.
- Marriages (Skæve): 1) Mette Hansdatter, 1810 (she died Mar 1812); 2) Maren Larsdatter, Nov 1812 (she died 19 Jul 1813, the day a daughter was born); 3) Ane Thomasdatter, 23 Jul 1813. **[reported, from Skæve register per researcher]**
- Children with Ane Thomasdatter (1834 census): Thomas 20, Mette 16, Christen 14, Inger 9.
- 1845, 1850: aftægtsmand (retired, on a retirement contract) at Lille Nejsum, "mandens fader", i.e. the householder's father.
- Died 25 Jul 1853, Skæve.

### Generation 5: Thomas Simonsen **[verified]**
- Born 16 Dec 1814 at Lille Nejsum; baptised at home 17 Dec; brought to Skæve church 15 Jan 1815; mother churched 12 Feb 1815.
- Parents: Gaardmand Simon Pedersen and Ane Thomasdatter, Lille Nejsum.
- Godparents: Jørgen Pedersen, farmer, Store Nejsum; Laurs Jensen, Stagsted; Otto Christensen, farmhand, Lille Nejsum; Ane Jensdatter, farmer's wife, Kraverbede; Inger Christensdatter, girl, Lille Nejsum.
- Named for his maternal grandfather Thomas.
- 1834 census: 20, at home. Probably householder of Lille Nejsum by 1845.
- Died 5 Dec 1898, Skæve. **[reported]**

### Generation 6: Christen (Kresten) Thomsen **[reported]**
- Born 7 Aug 1850, Skæve. Probably at Lille Nejsum, in the household with grandfather Simon.

### Generation 7: Jens Kristian Severin Thomsen Bøgh **[reported]**
- Born 22 Apr 1876, Klæstrup, Jerslev parish. The line returns to Jerslev parish after c. 175 years.

### Generation 8: Jens Kristian Marinus Thomsen Bøgh **[reported]**
- Born 1903, Klæstrup, Jerslev parish.
