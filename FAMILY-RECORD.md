# Family record: working notes

Running notes of every person, place and connection established in this research, kept so the
family history page can be written from sources rather than memory. Items marked **[verified]**
have been read from the original image. Items marked **[reported]** come from the family record on
Ancestry and have not yet been checked. Items marked **[hypothesis]** are inferences.

## People, by generation

### Generation 1: Søren Jensen **[verified]**
- Resident at Fredenborg, Svennum, Jerslev parish. "Sørens udj Fredenborg" 13 Feb 1698; "Søren Jensen Fredenborg" 10 Sep 1702.
- Children: Christen (bapt. 13 Feb 1698), Jens (bapt. 10 Sep 1702).
- Possibly the same as "Søren Jensen i Knæppen i Svennum" who baptised a son Jens 25 Nov 1708 (Knæppen a locality in Svennum).
- Not an Andersen brother; probably entered the house by marriage. **[hypothesis]**
- Possible father: Jens Sørensen, co-tenant of Svennum farm no. 6 in 1688, perhaps the Jens Sørensen who was farmhand to Anders and Niels Pedersen of Jerslev in 1653. **[hypothesis]**
- Birth and death not found. Burials to 1725 have no Søren Jensen of Fredenborg.

### The Andersen household at Fredenborg, 1676–1699 **[verified]**
- Anders "i Fredenborg", 1676 tithe case. Probably Anders Pedersen of Jerslev (fl. 1632–1654), son of Peder Pedersen, estate agent for the Kaas family. **[hypothesis]**
- Peder Andersen, 1676 Trøgdrup summons; child Niels buried 15 Jan 1688 "i Fredenborg i Svennum".
- Hans Andersen, son Christen (9) buried 1 Dec 1689 "i Fredenborg".
- Niels Andersen, tenant of Svennum no. 8 (the house) and no. 3 in 1688; byname "Niels Fredenborg" 1692, 1698; wife Maren Nielsdatter (36) buried 26 Sep 1689; children buried 1688, 1692, 1698; he himself probably the Niels Andersen "i Jerslef" buried 25 Dec 1719 aged 82 with wife Helle Jensdatter (55).
- Jens Andersen, tenant of Svennum no. 4 in 1688.
- Niels Tomsen, tenant of Svennum no. 2 in 1688; "Niels Tomsen Fredenborren" buried 7 May 1699 aged 80.
- Palle Nielsen "i Fredenborg", godfather 28 Jun 1704.

### Generation 2: Christen Sørensen **[baptism verified]**
- Baptised Jerslev 13 Feb 1698, son of Søren of Fredenborg. Godparents: Knud Kampmann (of Kølskegård, 1666–1704, son of the priest of Aggersborg), Kield Andersøn (possibly Kjeld Nors, parish clerk), Lars Nielsen (probably tenant of Svennum no. 5), Marie Fischer the priest's wife (1652–1717, wife of Frederik Boysen, priest 1684–1699), Dorthe Jensdatter (of the clerk's house).
- Death 20 Feb 1746 at "Fredensborg" **[reported]**: NOT in the Jerslev burials for 1744–1747. The report must come from another parish's register and another Fredensborg. Unresolved.

### Generation 3: Peder Jørgensen **[verified]** — formerly recorded as "Peder Sørensen"
- The 1787 census (Raunsholt, Skæve) wrote him "Peder Sørensen, 63". This was an enumerator's error. The Hellevad register and the 1801 census both give **Peder Jørgensen**.
- Baptised Jerslev 6 Nov 1729: "Peder, Jørgen Pedersens og Mette Pedersdatters i Villestrup". The scribe first wrote "Christen Jensens", struck it, and wrote "Jørgen Pedersen" above. Godparents: Christen i Sigaard, Jens i Høholt, Peder i Falden, Christens hustru i Krattet, Maren Jensdatter paa Veiene. (DFS kbid821338; image 14415867.) Identification with the Hellevad groom rests on the name, the 1801 age (71 → b. 1730) and the absence of any other Peder Jørgensen born 1720–1740 in indexed Hjørring amt registers. **[strong, one step short of direct proof]**
- Betrothed 11 Nov 1760, married 15 Dec 1760 at Hellevad to Inger Christensdatter: "ung Karl Peder Jørgensen af Nørkier[?] med Pigen Inger Christensdatter ibid."
- Resident at Hørkied (Hørkiær) in Kirkeskov, Hellevad parish, 1761–1763.
- At Raunsholt, Skæve, by 1787 (retired, son Christen holds the farm) and 1801 (husbonde again, aged 71, "bonde og gårdbeboer", one marriage).
- Death not yet found; after 1 Feb 1801, probably Skæve.
- Children: Jørgen (bapt. 8 Mar 1761, Hellevad; godparents Jens Andersen af Fodtved, Christen Christensen af Kirkeskov, Simon Christensen af Allerop; absent 1787/1801); Christen (bapt. 20 Nov 1763, Hellevad; carried by Maren Hansdatter fra Brønhavnen[?] i Voer Sogn; same three godfathers; farmer at Raunsholt 1787, single at home 1801); Mads (c. 1767); Anne Kristine (c. 1771); Mette Marie (c. 1774–76; at home 1801 as Mette, 27); Simon (c. 1777–78).
- 1801 household also: Christen Laursen, 32, married farmhand (possible son-in-law); Christen Christensen, 20; Jens Jensen, 12; Ane Jensdatter, 20, servants.

### Inger Christensdatter **[verified as wife and mother]**
- Born c. 1733 (53 in 1787; 68 in 1801). Birthplace not found; no Inger with a father Christen in Hellevad baptisms 1733–35 (the only Inger, 4th Advent 1733, is Morten Larsen's of Trøgdrup).
- Probable brother: Simon Christensen of Allerup, godfather 1761 and 1763. Possible sister: Else Christensdatter, wife of Niels Othesen of Allerup, 1734.
- Father's first name Christen (patronymic; second son named Christen). The Ancestry suggestion Christen Sørensen (c. 1692–1766) of Vognsild, Gislum herred, is 60–70 km away and rejected.
- Alive 1 Feb 1801 at Raunsholt.

### Generation 2: Jørgen Pedersen and Mette Pedersdatter of Villestrup **[verified; patronymic 2:1]**
- Betrothed 13 Apr 1727, married 26 Oct 1727, Jerslev. The marriage entry writes the groom **"Jørgen Jensen"**; both baptisms (1728, 1729) write **"Jørgen Pedersen"**. Taken as Pedersen on the two baptisms, with the 1727 form recorded as a scribal variant until a fourth record settles it. No home farm given for either party.
- Children:
  - **Kirsten**, bapt. 11 Feb 1728, Villestrup. Godparents: Jens i Kiøbakken, Christen Christensen i Jerslev, Peder Mylund i Jerslev, Christen Pedersens hustru i Krattet, Maren Pedersdatter i Smikken.
  - **Peder**, bapt. 6 Nov 1729, Villestrup (scribe first wrote "Christen Jensens", struck, corrected to Jørgen Pedersen). Godparents: Christen i Sigaard, Jens i Høholt, Peder i Falden, Christens hustru i Krattet, Maren Jensdatter paa Veiene.
  - No further children in Jerslev baptisms Jan 1727 – Feb 1733 (read in full).
- Not buried in Jerslev 1725–1734 (read in full); deaths later.
- **Christen Pedersen's wife of Krattet** stood godmother at both baptisms: Christen Pedersen of Krattet is the likely brother of Jørgen. Krattet (Sterupkrat, 1606) lies c. 0.5 km from Villestrup. Next thread: his baptisms, marriage and burial for the father's name.
- Villestrup: farm in Jerslev parish east of Sterup, 1662 "Willestrup". Neighbouring godparent farms: Sigård, Høholt, Falden, Krattet, Vejen, Smikken, Kiøbakken (Kjøbakken).
- Ancestry hint "Jørgen Pedersen b. 1689 Elsted, Århus, son of Peder Sørensen and Mette Clementsdatter" rejected: 170 km away and a decade too old for a first marriage in 1727.

### Generation 4: Simon Pedersen **[1814, 1834 verified; birth open]** — son of Peder Jørgensen and Inger Christensdatter (1787 census)
- Gaardmand (farmer) at Lille Nejsum, Skæve parish.
- Ages: 1834 census 60 (b. 1774); 1840 census 63, 1845 68, 1850 73, burial 1853 76 (all b. 1777). Birth year taken as 1777.
- Birthplace per 1845 and 1850 censuses: Hellevad sogn.
- Baptism NOT found in Hellevad 1776–Sep 1778, nor in Skæve 1776–Oct 1779. Ørum and Hellum pages of the Hellevad book not yet read; years before 1776 not read.
- 1787 census, Raunsholt, Skæve: Simon Pedersen, 9, "deres børn" (b. c. 1778), child of Peder [Jørgensen] and Inger Christensdatter. Not in the 1801 Raunsholt household (in service elsewhere, aged c. 23).
- Marriages (Skæve): 1) Mette Hansdatter, 1810 (she died Mar 1812); 2) Maren Larsdatter, Nov 1812 (she died 19 Jul 1813, the day a daughter was born); 3) Ane Thomasdatter, 23 Jul 1813. **[reported, from Skæve register per researcher]**
- Children with Ane Thomasdatter (1834 census): Thomas 20, Mette 16, Christen 14, Inger 9.
- 1845, 1850: aftægtsmand (retired, on a retirement contract) at Lille Nejsum, "mandens fader", i.e. the householder's father.
- Died 25 Jul 1853, Skæve.

### Generation 5: Thomas Simonsen **[verified]**
- Born 16 Dec 1814 at Lille Nejsum; baptised at home 17 Dec; brought to Skæve church 15 Jan 1815; mother churched 12 Feb 1815.
- Parents: Gaardmand Simon Pedersen and Ane Thomasdatter, Lille Nejsum.
- Godparents: Jørgen Pedersen, farmer, Store Nejsum; Laurs Jensen, Stagsted; Otto Christensen, farmhand, Lille Nejsum; Ane Jensdatter, farmer's wife, Kraverbede; Inger Christensdatter, girl, Lille Nejsum.
- Named for his maternal grandfather Thomas.
- 1834 census: 20, at home. Probably householder of Lille Nejsum by 1845.
- Died 5 Dec 1898, Skæve. **[reported]**

### Generation 6: Christen (Kresten) Thomsen **[reported]**
- Born 7 Aug 1850, Skæve. Probably at Lille Nejsum, in the household with grandfather Simon.

### Generation 7: Jens Kristian Severin Thomsen Bøgh **[reported]**
- Born 22 Apr 1876, Klæstrup, Jerslev parish. The line returns to Jerslev parish after c. 175 years.

### Generation 8: Jens Kristian Marinus Thomsen Bøgh **[reported]**
- Born 1903, Klæstrup, Jerslev parish.

## Open questions, in order of value
1. Christen Pedersen of Krattet (godparent household 1728–29): his records for the father's name, and to settle Jørgen's patronymic (Pedersen vs the 1727 "Jensen").
1b. Jørgen Pedersen and Mette Pedersdatter: burials after 1734; Kirsten Jørgensdatter's later life.
2. Peder Jørgensen's confirmation c. 1744–46 (Jerslev or Hellevad confirmation lists from 1737) and his burial after 1801 (Skæve).
3. Simon Pedersen's baptism c. 1777–78, now to be sought under father Peder Jørgensen: Hellevad 1776–78 reread for that name; Ørum/Hellum pages; Skæve 1778 reread.
4. Inger Christensdatter's baptism c. 1731–36 and parents (Hellevad, Ørum, Hellum).
5. The Fredenborg line (Søren Jensen, Christen Sørensen): a separate, documented family, no longer in the direct line; their story stays on the Finding Fredenborg page.
6. Thomas Simonsen's marriage and the 1850 Skæve census household at Lille Nejsum.
